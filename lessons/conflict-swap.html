Class code:

Your responses are anonymous. Nobody can trace this back to you.

Step 1 of 2 · Describe a real conflict you've experienced

Work, university, a team project, family — anything real. A strong write-up covers all four of these: (1) the background and the people involved (no real names), (2) what triggered the conflict, (3) how each side actually responded, and (4) how it ended, or where it stands now. Minimum 400 characters — a classmate has to be able to analyze this without asking you anything.

Submission received

Your ticket:

When everyone has submitted, your instructor will assign you a classmate's conflict to analyze. This screen updates automatically — keep it open.

Step 2 of 2 · Analyze this conflict

Read the conflict below carefully — you may be asked to walk the class through it. Each answer needs at least 80 characters; specific beats general.
