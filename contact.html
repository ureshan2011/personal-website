FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a free consultation?

Use the Book a Free Consultation link above (or go to yasassri.me/app/#/consult) to pick a 30–45 minute slot on education, research or career guidance — no charge, and no account needed.

How do I invite him to speak?

Use the Invite Me to Speak link above (yasassri.me/app/#/invite) to browse talk topics and send event details for a keynote, guest lecture, workshop or panel. Recent examples include the CODE with WIE 2026 workshop and an upcoming NZGDC 2026 talk.

How long does he take to respond?

Messages sent through the contact form below go straight to his inbox — no account needed. He is generally available Monday–Saturday, 10:00–17:00, and replies personally, though response time can vary with teaching and research commitments.

Is consulting with him paid or free?

The initial consultation booked via Book a Free Consultation (30–45 minutes, on education, research or career guidance) is free, with no charge. For larger engagements — extended advisory work, research collaboration or speaking — scope and any terms are discussed directly once you get in touch.

Does he supervise theses or capstone projects?

Yes. As a Senior Lecturer at Yoobee College of Creative Innovation and Postdoctoral Researcher at HIT Lab NZ, he supervises capstone and thesis projects, mainly in information systems, project management and immersive technology. Select “Project Mentoring” in the contact form’s subject field, or book office hours via the consultation link.

How do I propose a research collaboration?

Select “Research Collaboration” from the subject dropdown in the contact form below, or book a research-focused consultation at yasassri.me/app/#/consult?type=research. His research spans location-based AR, spatial computing and human-computer interaction, with industry partners including Sony Interactive Entertainment and Niantic.
