NZGDC 2026TALK TAKEAWAYS

Six rules for designing shared worlds across distance

From three multiplayer AR games and 128 players at HIT Lab NZ.

  1. If players can switch the AR off, it isn't a mechanic yet.Make something the game needs that only AR can do.
  2. Scale changes presence.Life-size to feel there. A window to get things done.
  3. Break physics on purpose.Players hid objects on the ceiling and in mid-air.
  4. A view-cone and voice go a long way.Try those before building full avatars.
  5. Players adapt to your controls first.Watch what they choose, not just how they score.
  6. Let players choose the pacing.An optional timer, toggles and a practice round.
