Philosophy

My approach to education is grounded in experiential and constructivist learning — I believe students learn best by building, failing, iterating, and reflecting. Whether teaching game development or human-computer interaction, I design environments where hands-on projects and real-world challenges take centre stage.

I prioritise purpose-driven education: helping students not just acquire technical skills, but develop the vision and initiative to use them meaningfully. If we can change the way students see the world, they will change the world they see.
