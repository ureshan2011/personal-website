    Yasas Wickramasinghe, PhD

    Postdoctoral Researcher · Senior Lecturer · Human Interface Technology

    Christchurch · Aotearoa New Zealand

    Download CV (PDF) ↗ Contact → LinkedIn ↗

    Profile

    Postdoctoral researcher and senior lecturer working at the intersection of immersive technology, software product engineering, and pedagogy. I lead research-informed teaching across postgraduate programmes and run mixed-methods user studies on multiplayer location-based AR, spatial interaction, and player experience — in partnership with Sony Interactive Entertainment, Niantic, and the University of Canterbury. My practice is built around experiential learning, industry collaboration, and values-driven, inclusive learning environments.

    Jun 2025 — Present

    Postdoctoral Researcher

    University of Canterbury — Human Interface Technology Lab NZ, Christchurch

    • Lead applied XR research in partnership with Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE), Japan, spanning design, implementation, and evaluation of VR applications.
    • Run mixed-methods studies on user experience, comfort, and usability in immersive environments.
    • Design multi-modal experimental platforms integrating XR Interaction Toolkit and OpenXR.
    • Mentor postgraduate students in experimental design, Unity development, and publication writing.

    Feb 2023 — Present

    Senior Lecturer

    Yoobee College of Creative Innovation, Christchurch

    • Independently designed and built an AI-first, learner-friendly interactive LMS for blended postgraduate delivery — video tutorials, gamified lessons and challenges, AI-driven assessments, and a business-intelligence dashboard giving the lecturer real-time cohort analytics.
    • Teach masters-level courses: Strategic Information Systems Planning, Database Management, and IT Project Management.
    • Supervise capstone projects; coordinate teaching assistants; lead moderation and quality assurance aligned with NZQA standards.
    • Recognised for innovative, high-engagement, research-informed teaching methods.
