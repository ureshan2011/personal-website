About

I'm a researcher and educator working on how AI and immersive technologies change higher education. My PhD examined augmented reality in HCI; my postdoctoral work at HIT Lab NZ runs immersion and presence studies in collaboration with Sony Interactive Entertainment. I teach Masters-level business informatics at Yoobee Colleges, where I've built my own learning platform to experiment with AI-augmented teaching at scale.

Before academia, I led engineering teams at 99X Technology in Sri Lanka and co-founded Navitaz VR Labs. I write about teaching with AI, building education products, and the practical realities of doing research and shipping software at the same time. I'm based in Christchurch, New Zealand, and maintain strong ties to Sri Lanka.
