Biography &
Interests

Dr. Yasas Sri Wickramasinghe is a transdisciplinary researcher whose work centres on location-based augmented reality games and spatial computing systems. He earned his PhD in Human Interface Technology from the University of Canterbury, New Zealand, where his doctoral research explored the design and evaluation of AR games in real-world environments.

His research bridges human-computer interaction, game design, and immersive technology. As a Co-Founder of NavitaZ VR Labs (MIT Global Startup Labs, 2015), he built AR/VR products long before these technologies entered the mainstream. Today, as a Postdoctoral Researcher at HIT Lab NZ and Senior Lecturer at Yoobee College of Creative Innovation, he combines cutting-edge research with a passion for inspiring the next generation of technology creators.
