New Zealand Scholarships 2027: Deadlines Sri Lankans Can't Miss

Want to study in New Zealand in 2027? Here are the scholarships open now at UC, Auckland, Waikato, Victoria and AUT, with the real dates and amounts, and what to do first.

In brief

  • UC, Auckland and Waikato have 2027 scholarship deadlines between 15 October and 30 November 2026.
  • UC gives $15,000 to students who finished school overseas, including Sri Lankan A/L students. It closes 31 October.
  • Victoria gives $15,000 (bachelor's) or $10,000 (master's) automatically with your offer. There's no separate form.
  • Most scholarships need an offer from the university first. So apply for your course now.
  • A New Zealand master's or PhD can lead to a work visa of up to three years, and it counts towards residence. This is general information, not immigration advice.

Thinking about studying in New Zealand? Planning to look into it "after the new year"?

Please don't wait. Most of the scholarship money for 2027 gets decided before Christmas. And most Sri Lankan students only find out after it's gone.

Here's what's open right now. All amounts are in New Zealand dollars.

  • 15 October: UC Doctoral Scholarship. $32,650 a year plus fees. That's tomorrow.
  • 20 October: University of Auckland. Have a master's offer by this date to get invited to its International Student Excellence Scholarship (25% of a year's fees). The form is then due 4 November.
  • 27 October: UC Commitments Doctoral Scholarships. Same $32,650 a year plus fees, plus $5,000 for research costs. These are tied to set PhD projects.
  • 31 October: UC International First Year Scholarship. $15,000. It's for students who finished school outside New Zealand, so Sri Lankan A/L students can apply.
  • 30 November: University of Waikato, Vice-Chancellor's International Excellence Scholarship. Up to $15,000, if you're starting in early 2027.

Two more worth knowing:

  • Victoria University of Wellington gives new international students $15,000 for a bachelor's or $10,000 for a master's. No separate form. It comes with your offer.
  • AUT has a scholarship for South Asia, and Sri Lanka is on the list. But AUT hadn't posted its 2027 closing date when I checked.

I checked every date and amount on the universities' own websites on 14 October 2026.

Why I'm writing this

I came to New Zealand from Sri Lanka and did my PhD here. Since then, a lot of Sri Lankan students have messaged me. I keep hearing the same thing:

"Aiyo, I only found out about the scholarship after it closed."

It's not that they weren't good enough. It's the timing. Here, the university year starts in February. So scholarships are decided in October and November. Back home, most of us start searching in January. By then it's too late.

So I put everything in one place. There's still time, but not much.

What's open right now

University of Canterbury (UC), Christchurch

  • International First Year Scholarship: $15,000 off your fees. It's for international students who finished school overseas. If you've done your A/Ls and meet UC's entry requirements, this one's for you. Closes 31 October.
  • Doctoral Scholarship: $32,650 a year plus fees, for up to three years. This round closes 15 October 2026. Missed it? The next round opens 20 March and closes 15 May 2027.
  • Commitments Doctoral Scholarships: $32,650 a year plus fees, plus $5,000 for research costs. These are for set PhD projects that UC advertises. Close 27 October.

UC also has $5,000 High Achievers Scholarships. But they're only for India, China and Southeast Asia. Sri Lanka isn't included. That's why the First Year Scholarship is the one to go for.

AUT, Auckland

  • International Scholarship, South Asia. Sri Lanka is on the list. In AUT's last round (for early 2026), it was $5,000 for a bachelor's, $10,000 if you had a B+ average or better, and $7,000 for postgrad. AUT lists this scholarship for early 2027 too, but its own site didn't show a closing date or the new amounts when I checked. Last time it stayed open until the places were filled. So apply early, and check AUT's page.

University of Auckland

  • International Student Excellence Scholarship: 25% of one year's fees, for new master's students. You can't apply for this one directly. Here's how it works. If you hold an offer for early 2027 by 20 October 2026, they invite you on 21 October. Then you fill in a short form by 4 November.

This is the one people miss the most. No offer by 20 October means no invite.

Victoria University of Wellington

  • VUW Undergraduate International Scholarship: $15,000 off your first-year fees, for new international students starting a bachelor's.
  • VUW Postgraduate International Scholarship: $10,000 off your fees, for a master's or a postgraduate diploma.

The best part: you don't apply separately. If you're eligible, Victoria tells you in your offer letter. There's no extra deadline. It closes when applications for your course close.

(You might see the old Tongarewa Scholarship on other websites. Victoria stopped offering it from 2026.)

University of Waikato, Hamilton and Tauranga

  • Vice-Chancellor's International Excellence Scholarship: up to $15,000, for bachelor's and taught postgrad students. If your offer is for early 2027, apply by 30 November. They look at applications every month, so earlier is better.

Which one should you go for?

It depends on where you are right now.

  • Just done your A/Ls (or doing them now)? You'll want a bachelor's. Look at UC First Year (31 October), Waikato (30 November), Victoria (automatic) and AUT South Asia.
  • Already have a bachelor's? You'll want a master's. Get your Auckland application in before 20 October. Then look at Waikato, Victoria (automatic) and AUT South Asia.
  • Have a master's or a good honours degree, and a research idea? You'll want a PhD. Your first step is finding a supervisor. Start emailing now. Then aim for UC's May 2027 round, or look at UC's project-based Commitments scholarships.

The mistake almost everyone makes

They wait to apply for the course. Don't.

Apply to the university first. Almost every scholarship here needs an offer. Auckland only invites students who already have one. Victoria gives its scholarship inside the offer letter. AUT and Waikato also need an offer for 2027.

Universities need time to check your transcripts, A/L results and English test. If you only apply for the course near the scholarship deadline, it's already too late. Do both now.

What about a full scholarship?

You might have heard of the Manaaki New Zealand Scholarships. They're paid by the New Zealand Government and cover fees, living costs and flights.

Sadly, Sri Lanka isn't on the eligible country list right now. It's open to Pacific countries and a few Asian countries (Cambodia, Indonesia, Lao PDR, Malaysia, Nepal, Philippines, Thailand, Timor-Leste and Viet Nam). The list can change, so it's worth checking now and then.

For a PhD, the closest thing to full funding is a university doctoral scholarship, like UC's $32,650 a year plus fees.

What about staying and working after?

A lot of people who message me aren't just thinking about a degree. They're thinking about their future. So here's how it works, based on Immigration New Zealand's website in October 2026.

  1. While you study. If your student visa allows work, you can work up to 25 hours a week. PhD and research master's students have no limit.
  2. After you graduate. Finish a New Zealand master's or PhD (at least 30 weeks of full-time study in that programme, here in New Zealand). Then you can get a Post-Study Work Visa for up to three years. You only get one. Apply within three months of your student visa ending (six months for a PhD). A postgraduate diploma can get you a one-year visa. And from 16 November 2026, a Level 7 graduate diploma can too, if you also have a bachelor's.
  3. Residence. From 24 August 2026, the Skilled Migrant Category gives 6 points for a New Zealand master's (if you also have a bachelor's and studied here for 30 weeks or more). A PhD also gets 6 points. But you still need a skilled job in New Zealand at the right pay, and you need to meet all the other rules.

So a New Zealand postgrad degree is a good start. But it's a start, not a promise.

What to do in the next four weeks

If you only remember one thing from this post, make it this list.

  1. This week: apply for your course. Pick two or three programmes. Send your applications. Get certified copies of your transcripts and A/L certificates.
  2. Week 2: get your documents ready. No IELTS or PTE score? Book a test. Ask two referees, and give them a clear date.
  3. Week 3: write your statement. Answer the actual question. Keep it simple: one goal, one real story, one plan for after.
  4. Week 4: send it in. Submit at least two days early. Save every confirmation email. Put the next round in your calendar, just in case.

Frequently asked questions

Which New Zealand scholarships can Sri Lankan students apply for now for 2027?

As of 14 October 2026: UC's International First Year Scholarship ($15,000, closes 31 October), Auckland's International Student Excellence Scholarship for master's students (25% of one year's fees; you need an offer by 20 October, then a form by 4 November), Waikato's Vice-Chancellor's International Excellence Scholarship (up to $15,000; apply by 30 November for early 2027), and Victoria's new international scholarships ($15,000 for a bachelor's, $10,000 for a master's or postgraduate diploma), which are given automatically with your offer. AUT also has a South Asia scholarship that includes Sri Lanka, but it hadn't published its 2027 closing date on its own website when I checked. Always confirm on the university's website.

Do I need an offer before I apply for a scholarship?

For most of them, yes. Auckland only invites master's students who have an offer by 20 October 2026. Victoria gives its scholarship with the offer itself. AUT and Waikato also need an offer. So apply for your course first, or at the same time.

Is there a full scholarship for Sri Lankans?

Not from the New Zealand Government right now. The Manaaki New Zealand Scholarships cover fees, living costs and flights, but Sri Lanka is not on the eligible country list. For a PhD, university doctoral scholarships like UC's ($32,650 a year plus fees) are the closest thing to full funding.

Can I work while I study in New Zealand?

Immigration New Zealand says that if your student visa allows work, you can work up to 25 hours a week. PhD and research master's students have no limit. Your own visa conditions decide what applies to you.

Does a New Zealand master's lead to residence?

Not automatically. A New Zealand master's or PhD (with at least 30 weeks of full-time study in the programme here) can get you a Post-Study Work Visa for up to three years. Under the Skilled Migrant Category, a New Zealand master's (with 30 weeks of full-time study here and a bachelor's) or a doctorate gives 6 points. You still need a skilled job here and must meet all the other rules. This is general information, not immigration advice. Check immigration.govt.nz or talk to a licensed immigration adviser.
