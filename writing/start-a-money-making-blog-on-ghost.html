First, find a niche that interests you and is relevant to your money blogging audience. Second, create high-quality content that will draw readers in and keep them coming back. Finally, monetize your blog through advertising and affiliate marketing related to your content. Below are the top-performing money making blog topics in making money online blogging.

You need to be smart enough to publish high-quality authentic content to attract and retain an audience around your blog. When you have a blog idea, now it’s time to think of a suitable domain name.

Domain: Google SEO Oriented Web URL for Money Blogging

The power of a domain name is highly underrated but it plays a main role in any money blog. Your domain name should match the niche you focus on, and this domain name will be the one that you will share on social media, such as facebook twitter linkedin youtube, to perform search engine optimization and even to promote your blog across other websites.

I personally recommend selecting and purchasing a domain name even before you start blog. There are many domain name providers out there, but if you use Namecheap, you can get a high-level domain name for a very reasonable price. And also, if you are a college student or a university student, you are eligible for a FREE Namecheap domain via GitHub Student Developer Pack.

Always pick an easy-to-remember, relevant and short domain name for your blog.

Blog: Best Content Management System (CMS) for Making Money Blog

It’s setup time! You need to configure Ghost CMS. There are two options for you. Either you can use Ghost Pro (Hosting is not required from yourend) or use the Ghost Self-hosting option. I recommend the second option because it is much more cheap and even it can be free if you are a college or university student.

Ghost is a state-of-the-art content management service similar to WordPress, but according to my experience, Ghost is way more fast and easy to set up. You do not need to spend days to get this up and running. And also Ghost provides amazing themes to select.

Ghost itself is free, and you need to use a hosting space to self-host it. Therefore, let’s see how to host Ghost CMS on our own hosting space.
