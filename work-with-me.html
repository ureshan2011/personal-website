Offer 01

XR Opportunity Sprint

For: tourism operators, museums and cultural sites, retailers, training providers and game studios considering AR or VR.

Most immersive projects go wrong before a line of code is written: they pick a use case where a phone screen would have done the job, or they bolt AR on as decoration. In two to three weeks we work out whether your idea deserves a budget, what the smallest convincing version looks like, and how you'll know it worked.

Built on three years designing and evaluating multiplayer location-based AR with Sony Interactive Entertainment, a Niantic developer accelerator grant, and co-founding NavitaZ VR Labs under the MIT Global Startup Labs programme. Read the case study →

What you get

  • Use-case assessment — where AR/VR genuinely beats a screen for your people, and where it doesn't
  • Experience concept with an interaction flow grounded in published design guidelines
  • Technology recommendation — WebAR or app, Unity, OpenXR, Niantic Lightship, device reach
  • Prototype plan and build estimate your team or vendor can act on
  • Evaluation plan so the pilot produces evidence, not opinions

Offer 02

Immersive UX & Playtest Evaluation

For: XR start-ups, game studios and product teams with a prototype or a live AR/VR experience.

You have a build. Is it comfortable? Do people understand it? Do they feel present, connected and engaged — or motion-sick? I design and run studies that answer those questions with evidence your designers can act on, using the same methods I publish at IEEE VR, ACM VRST and OzCHI.

Built on three published user studies with 128 participants on remote multiplayer AR, a 36-participant study on trust in shared AR (ACM SUI 2024), and a VRST 2026 paper on how display type and camera perspective shape VR presence and motion sickness. See the design playbook →

What you get

  • Study design matched to your question — lab study, in-the-wild playtest, or a comparison of design variants
  • Established measures of presence, simulator sickness, player experience, workload and usability, plus behavioural and interview data
  • Session protocol and recruitment, including safety for players moving through physical space
  • Analysis with prioritised recommendations — what to fix first, and why
  • A findings session with your designers and developers
Duration3–6 weeks
FormatIn person or remote
You receiveReport + session

EdTech & Engaged-Classroom Advisory

For: private colleges, training providers, universities and EdTech teams.

Students don't skip a class that feels like play and gives them something real to build. I design and build the lessons and platforms that make that happen in my own postgraduate teaching — and help institutions do the same, without trading rigour for engagement.

Built on YooBees, the learning platform I built for 228 students from 24 countries; four postgraduate courses rebuilt as 39 interactive lessons, labs and study packs; the MOOC platform I scaled to 150,000+ learners; and ICT Campus, the bilingual online school I founded. Read the case study →

How an engagement runs

Discovery call

Thirty minutes, free. We talk through your goal, constraints and timeline. If I'm not the right fit, I'll say so and point you to someone who is.

Written scope

A short proposal: deliverables, timeline, fee and what I'll need from your side. Agreed before any work starts.

Working sessions

Regular sessions with your team, with progress you can see every week — not a reveal at the end.

Handover

Everything written up so your team can carry it forward without me, and I stay reachable for questions afterwards.

FAQ

Before we talk

Do you work with organisations outside New Zealand?

Yes. Most engagements run remotely, with working sessions over video. I'm based in Christchurch and can work in person anywhere in New Zealand; travel further afield is by arrangement.

How are fees set?

Each engagement is scoped in writing, with deliverables, timeline and a fixed fee agreed before any work starts. The first discovery call is free and carries no obligation.

Is this work connected to the University of Canterbury or Yoobee College?

No. Consulting, workshops and speaking are my own independent practice. They don't speak for, and aren't offered on behalf of, the University of Canterbury, HIT Lab NZ or Yoobee College of Creative Innovation.

Can we talk under an NDA?

Yes. I'm happy to work under a non-disclosure agreement, and to sign one before you share project details.

I'm a student. Can I still get help?

Of course. Students get free guidance on study, research and careers through the consultation booking on my platform, and there is a separate free advice form for anyone planning a master's or PhD in New Zealand.
