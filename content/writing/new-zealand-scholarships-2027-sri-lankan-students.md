If you've been telling yourself you'll look into studying in New Zealand "after the new year", this is the article I wish someone had sent me.

**Most of the scholarship money for 2027 is decided before Christmas 2026.** Five New Zealand universities have rounds open right now, and every one of them closes in the next seven weeks:

- **15 October:** UC Doctoral Scholarship, worth $32,650 a year plus fees. That's tomorrow.
- **31 October:** UC International First Year Scholarship, $15,000, for students who finished school outside New Zealand. That includes Sri Lankan A/L students.
- **1 November:** Victoria University of Wellington's Tongarewa Scholarship, up to $10,000.
- **4 November:** University of Auckland International Student Excellence Scholarship, 25% of a year's tuition for master's students. You need an offer by **20 October** to be invited.
- **22 November:** AUT International Scholarship for South Asia, **which names Sri Lanka**: $10,000 for a bachelor's, $7,000 for postgraduate study.
- **30 November:** University of Waikato Vice-Chancellor's International Excellence Scholarship, up to $15,000.

All amounts are in New Zealand dollars. I checked every date on the university's own page on 14 October 2026. The one exception is AUT's closing date, which comes from StudySpy because AUT's page hadn't published it yet.

## Why I'm writing this

I came to New Zealand from Sri Lanka, did my PhD here, and have spent years since helping students find their way into postgraduate study. Every year I hear the same story from Sri Lankan students: *"I only found out about the scholarship after it closed."*

That's not because they weren't good enough. It's because New Zealand's academic year runs from February, so the money is decided in October and November, while most of us are still thinking about the year ahead. By the time a student in Colombo, Kandy or Galle starts searching in January, the main rounds are over.

So here's the whole picture in one place, while there's still time to act on it.

## The rounds open right now

### University of Canterbury (UC), Christchurch

- **UC International First Year Scholarship (Offshore): $15,000** towards tuition. For international students who completed their university entrance qualification overseas, which can include Sri Lankan A/L students who meet UC's entry requirements. **Closes 31 October.**
- **UC Doctoral Scholarship: $32,650 a year plus thesis tuition fees**, for up to three years. The October round closes **15 October 2026**. If you miss it, the next round opens 20 March and closes 15 May 2027.

UC also runs High Achievers Scholarships ($5,000) for students from India, China and Southeast Asia. Sri Lanka isn't on that list, which is exactly why the First Year Scholarship matters.

### Auckland University of Technology (AUT)

- **AUT International Scholarship, South Asia: $10,000 for undergraduate, $7,000 for postgraduate.** Sri Lanka is named among the eligible countries. It's for students with an offer to start in Semester 1 or Semester 2 of 2027. **Closes 22 November 2026** (dates from StudySpy; check AUT's own scholarship page as it updates).

### University of Auckland

- **International Student Excellence Scholarship: 25% of one year's tuition**, for new international master's students. There is no open application. Instead, students who hold a programme offer for Semester One or Quarter Two 2027 **by 20 October 2026** are invited on 21 October and must return a short form **by 4 November**.

This is the one people miss most. If your master's application to Auckland isn't in yet, the invitation won't come.

### Victoria University of Wellington | Te Herenga Waka

- **Tongarewa International Scholarship: up to $10,000** (awarded as $2,500, $5,000, $7,500 or $10,000), for new international students entering their first year of an undergraduate or postgraduate programme. **Trimester 1 applications close 1 November at 4.30 pm** (New Zealand time).

### University of Waikato, Hamilton and Tauranga

- **Vice-Chancellor's International Excellence Scholarship: up to $15,000**, for international students starting undergraduate or taught postgraduate study. For a Trimester A (early 2027) offer, apply by **30 November**. Applications are reviewed monthly, so earlier is better.

## Which one is yours?

Where you are right now decides which door to knock on:

- **Just finished, or sitting, your A/Ls?** You're looking at a bachelor's. Start with UC's First Year Scholarship, AUT South Asia, Waikato and Tongarewa.
- **Already have a bachelor's?** You're looking at a master's. Get your Auckland application in before 20 October, then AUT South Asia, Waikato and Tongarewa.
- **Have a master's or a strong honours degree, and a research idea?** You're looking at a PhD. The real first step is a supervisor who wants you. Start emailing now, and aim for UC's May 2027 round.

![Three columns: A/L leavers, bachelor's holders and research-ready students, each with the scholarships that fit](https://www.yasassri.me/assets/images/blog/nz-scholarships-2027/fig-3-which-fits.webp "Find your lane. Almost every award needs an offer of place first.")

## The one rule that catches everyone

**Apply for admission first.** Almost every scholarship above is tied to an offer of place. Auckland only invites students who already hold an offer. AUT and Waikato attach their awards to an offer for a 2027 programme.

Universities need time to assess Sri Lankan transcripts, A/L results and English test scores. If you wait for the scholarship deadline before applying for the programme, you've already missed it. Do both now.

## What about a fully funded scholarship?

The **Manaaki New Zealand Scholarships**, funded by the New Zealand Government, cover tuition, living costs and travel for students from eligible countries. Applications usually open in March and close in April, for study starting the following year. Massey University lists the next window as 1 March to 10 April 2027.

Manaaki is competitive and comes with obligations, including returning home after your studies. Check the official Manaaki site to confirm whether Sri Lanka is eligible in that round and which fields are prioritised. If it is, start preparing now. Five months goes quickly when you need referees and a strong development statement.

## The long game: study, work, residence

Many readers who message me aren't only thinking about a degree. They're thinking about a future. So here's how Immigration New Zealand's published settings connect, as of October 2026:

1. **While you study.** A student visa that allows work lets you work **up to 25 hours a week**. PhD and research master's students have **no limit on hours**.
2. **When you graduate.** A New Zealand master's or doctorate, with at least 30 weeks of full-time study in New Zealand, can lead to a **Post-Study Work Visa of up to three years**. You only get one, and you apply within three months of your student visa ending (six months for a doctorate). From 16 November 2026, an eligible Level 8 postgraduate diploma can also lead to a one-year visa.
3. **Towards residence.** From 24 August 2026, the **Skilled Migrant Category** gives **6 points** for a New Zealand master's degree (with a bachelor's and at least 30 weeks of study in New Zealand) or for a doctorate. You still need skilled work in New Zealand at the required wage and must meet every other requirement.

In other words, a New Zealand postgraduate degree is one of the clearer starting points. It's a starting point, though, not a guarantee.

## Your next four weeks

If you take one thing from this article, let it be this plan:

1. **This week: get an offer moving.** Shortlist two or three programmes and apply for admission. Order certified copies of your transcripts and A/L certificates.
2. **Week 2: gather your evidence.** Book IELTS or PTE if you don't have a valid score. Ask two referees and give them a firm date.
3. **Week 3: write the statement.** Answer the question they actually ask. One clear goal, one real story, one plan for what you'll do afterwards.
4. **Week 4: submit and track.** Submit at least two days early, keep every confirmation email, and put the next round in your calendar in case this one slips past.
