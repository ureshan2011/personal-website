The Collaboration Reflex

Most teams reach for “let's find the win-win” by default. Using real, anonymised conflicts, participants learn to read which response a conflict actually needs — then analyse each other's anonymised conflicts in a structured swap.

  • Read a conflict with four practical frameworks before responding
  • Separate the root cause from the surface argument
  • Name the mode that was used — and the one that should have been

Technology & data

For product, engineering and analytics teams — and for the managers who work with them.

2–3 hoursLaptop eachDevelopers, QA & product

Think like an attacker: the SwiftShop lab

A deliberately insecure online shop with twenty hidden vulnerabilities — editable price tags, secrets in the page source, XSS, privilege escalation and business-logic flaws. Teams find them, exploit them safely, then explain the fix.

  • Spot common web vulnerabilities from the attacker's side
  • See the business-logic flaws that automated scanners miss
  • Leave with a fix-it checklist for your own product

How it works

Tailored to your team, kept after the day

Tailored

Your context, not a template

Before the session we talk through your team, your goals and the examples that will land. Workshops can be combined into a full day or split across shorter sessions.

Hands-on

Doing, not watching

Every workshop is built around activities participants complete themselves — best with groups of roughly 8 to 30. Larger groups can work as a keynote plus breakout sessions.

Lasting

Materials stay open

The lessons behind each workshop are free online, so participants can go back to them — and share them with colleagues — long after the day.

i

Formats & fees. In person anywhere in New Zealand and online worldwide. Workshops are paid engagements; the fee depends on length, group size and travel, and is agreed up front. Consulting and training are my own independent practice, separate from my university and college roles.
